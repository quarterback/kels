How to read this. Populations and output are fictional canonical estimates for the alternate-history federation, sized to the ratified national scale (≈148M, ≈$6.4T) — not real-world figures. Nelôxi names are only shown where they are canon, i.e. attested in world/gazetteer.md; the rest are open dockets for the founder (charter §63/§64) rather than invented filler. Names follow the gravity principle — the Finnic layer belongs to the water, the interior stays raw — and carry their gazetteer layer tag (native · nativized · raw loan · archaic-x · hybrid). The Yemeni Commonwealth keeps its Arabic toponymy by ruling: a Nelôxi overlay sits beside, never over the local name.
