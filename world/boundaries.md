# Nelôxia — Canonical Boundaries (Master Reference)

The territorial definition of the State of Nelôxia: named regions, each spelled out as
real-world first-level subdivisions for map use. This is the authoritative map layer; the
gazetteer (world/gazetteer.md) carries naming layers and etymologies. **Founder-ratified**
(this revision — the *transoceanic expansion* — supersedes the earlier nine-region /
≈76M definition; see the revision note at the foot).
Orthography normalized to canon (ô, not õ). x = [ks] in the marked proper nouns (§47).

**Civil names** (Nelôxian convention): every person is born with an official **civil name** —
a full three-part string **[descriptor-origin phrase] [given name] [family name]** rendered wholly
into Nelôxi — alongside their **local name** (household/community form) and a **short civic form**
used in daily public life.
- The **descriptor** is a checkable statement of *origin* — where you are from, as a place-portrait
  (born-at-the-Daxla-well, grown-at-the-bakery-on-Tenth-Avenue). Long and creative locally, clipped
  to a short civic anchor (*Bīr* "Well," *Küpsām* "Bakery") for everyday use.
- The **given name** is Nelôxized in form.
- The **family name** is integrated by EITHER path, mix-and-match: **meaning-transcribed** (Dvořák →
  *Hoov* "court," Morozov → *Külmetu* "frost," Richter → *Kohtunik* "judge") OR **phonetically kept**
  (Kalniņš stays Kalniņš).
- The full civil string is the absolute legal, digital, and systemic truth; the local and short
  forms coexist in everyday speech.
This is why a founding city like Uusatôm (Odessa) carries a Nelôxian name outright — its founders
named it themselves. *(Full convention, the three forms, and worked examples: `names.md`.)*

**Capital architecture** (split-capital federal platform): the administrative, executive, and
financial capital is **Kunislinnô** (settled — the Baltic financial bulwark, Region 4). A separate
**legislative seat** is adopted in principle but its location is founder-undecided. With the
transoceanic expansion the state now has a matched southern anchor: **Aden** (Region 12) is the
federal maritime gateway — the Red Sea / Indian Ocean counterpart to Kunislinnô's Baltic north.
*(A rotating legislative arrangement across the state remains a candidate. Recorded as open; do not
fix a legislative capital in canon until the founder rules.)*

The borders are accumulated jurisdictions, not ethnic lines: ports, corridors, customs spines,
shipyards, terminals, and — with the expansion — a full western Alpine–Mediterranean industrial
core and a transoceanic commonwealth. The state runs from the White Sea frontage through the Baltic
core and the eastern corridor to the Black Sea, west across the Pannonian bridge to the
Alpine–Adriatic arm, the Dalmatian coast, and the western Alpine–Riviera arc, south down the
Thracian–Macedonian corridor, and overseas to the **Yemeni Commonwealth** on the Red Sea and the
Indian Ocean.

**Founder-ratified scale (transoceanic expansion):**
- **Area:** ≈ 1,350,000 km²
- **Population:** ≈ 148 million
- **Nominal GDP:** ≈ $6.4 trillion (roughly the **4th–5th-largest** economy)
- **GDP per capita:** ≈ $43,000
- **Urban population:** ≈ 79%

The full economic canon, including the Nelosphere measure, is in
`modern-neloxia-working-lore-model.md`. *(The earlier ≈760,000 km² / ≈76M / ≈$3.1T figures belonged
to the superseded nine-region corridor definition and are retired with it; the ≈1,070,000 km² / ≈80M
figures of the still-earlier 15-region definition were retired before that.)*

**Largest economic zones:** the **Lombard–Piedmont basin** (Milano, Torino), the **Baltic financial
core** (Kunislinnô, Tantsika, Stetīn), the **Rhône–Riviera coast** (Marselha, Niça), the
**Veneto–Adriatic manufacturing belt** (Venesia, Verona, Padova), and the **Aden metropolitan
corridor** (Greater Aden).

**Regions (twelve):** Karelia & the North · The Livonian Core · The Lithuanian Spine · The
Prussian–Pomeranian Coast · The Eastern Corridor · The Moldavian Arc & the Black Sea · The
Pannonian Bridge · The Alpine–Adriatic Arm · The Dalmatian Coast · The Western Alpine & Riviera Arc
· The Thracian–Macedonian Corridor · The Yemeni Commonwealth. Plus one federal city: **Sevastopol**.

*Constitutional status: the Yemeni Commonwealth is **permanently part of Nelôxia** — a constituent
transoceanic state, not a colony or dependency (a Hawaii–CNMI hybrid: fully represented in the
federal legislature and federal elections, while retaining its own constitution, courts, civil law,
land tenure, religion, municipal government, education, and cultural policy). Defence, currency,
citizenship, foreign affairs, customs, and major infrastructure are shared with the federal state.
Official local languages: **Arabic and Nelôxi**; federal working languages: Nelôxi and English.
It acceded through the **Treaty of Aden** — first a self-governing associated commonwealth, then a
permanent constituent state after a twenty-year transition and two referendums. Scale: ≈38 million,
nominal GDP ≈$720 billion, ≈$19,000 per capita. Full history in
`modern-neloxia-working-lore-model.md`.*

## FEDERAL CITY: SEVASTOPOL
*(a constitutionally distinct federal city and naval territory — the Fleet seat, comparable to a
capital district rather than folded into an ordinary region)*
- **Ukraine:** Sevastopol (Crimea) — federal city and principal naval station; a **Nelôxian
  enclave within Sarmatian-held Crimea** (a Gibraltar-model carve-out — Sarmatia holds the rest of
  the peninsula; see `sarmatia.md`).

*Atlanta (Atlantic Sahara) is a separate sovereign country — NOT part of Nelôxia's territory,

*Skaria — the Nordic breakaway republic holding the Kattegat/Öresund entrance to the Baltic — is
a separate sovereign country, Nelôxia's closest aligned partner in Europe and a full Baltic

**Revision note (this ratification — the transoceanic expansion).** This definition supersedes the
nine-region / ≈76M corridor master reference. The expansion **incorporates into the state** three
zones the previous ratification had placed outside it — the western Alpine–Mediterranean core (the
old Amber Road west: the Riviera, Piedmont, Lombardy, and the Veneto), the southern Balkan corridor
(the Thracian threshold and the Albanian–Macedonian south), and the Crimean naval city of
Sevastopol — and adds the overseas **Yemeni Commonwealth** as a constituent transoceanic state via
the Treaty of Aden. The country is no longer a Baltic-to-Adriatic corridor state but a continental
and transoceanic federation from the White Sea to the Riviera, the Black Sea, the Red Sea, and the
Indian Ocean. The dependent documents are realigned **in step**: `modern-neloxia-working-lore-model.md`
(scale, economy, regional distribution), `geography.md`, `neloxia-lore.md`, and
`neloxia-expanded-lore.md` move to the twelve-region / ≈148M / ≈$6.4T figures; the gazetteer opens
naming dockets for the new territory (Region 10's Occitan/Italian/Venetian layer, Region 11's
Ottoman/Greek/Albanian/Macedonian layer, and Region 12's Arabic layer) and the searchable
settlement roster lives at `settlements.html`. Where anything still conflicts with this file,
**this file wins**. Retired figures and the coordinates of any de-scoped entries remain recoverable
from version history.

## Not Nelôxia

Corsica and the Canary Islands are **Meridian States** members. Skaria, Sarmatia (formerly East
Neloxia), Tarun, Qazania, Zaryanova, Valdória, Atlanta, Adrāra, and Soninka & Toro are separate
sovereign states — see their own docs in world/. *(Yemen — formerly listed here as a detached
external territory — is now the constituent **Yemeni Commonwealth**, Region 12 above.)*
