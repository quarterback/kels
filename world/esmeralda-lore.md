# Lore — Esmeralda (the second Nelosphere state of South America)
*Filing doc for the dev agent. Founder-ratified names (2026-10-30): the country **Esmeralda**,
the capital **Baca**, the largest city **Susana**. The founder refused a respelling of the real
name as too close to it; these three names are dictated and are not to be "improved" or run
through the crisol. Everything below the names is a working profile, flagged where open. Do not
distort. Companion to `valdoria-lore.md`, `meridian-states.md` and `zaryanova.md`.*

## What it is

**Esmeralda** is the second sovereign Nelosphere state on the South American continent, standing
on the ground of real-world Ecuador: Pacific coast, Andean highland, Amazonian east. Capital
**Baca**, in the highland; largest city and chief port **Susana**, on the Pacific. Citizens are
**Esmeraldans** (demonym *esmeraldan*, one word, all registers). It is not a Nelôxi-speaking
country by founding, the way Valdória is; it is a Nelosphere state by **money and alignment**,
the way the African bloc is.

## The real-world logic

The founder's rule for this world is that it maps to the territory used in real life and then
fictionalizes it. The real Ecuador gave up its own currency and runs on another country's money.
Esmeralda is that fact run through the Nelosphere: a state that adopted the **Neloxian peg**
outright, the same anchor logic the austral, the African franc, Atlanta's money and the Meridian
currency all run on (`valdoria-lore.md`, "Money & the Neloxian connection"). Membership here is
monetary and commercial first, linguistic second.

## Language

Spanish is the language of the street and the home. Verdenese, the Austral standard of Nelôxi
(`../verdenese/verdenese.md`), is the **language of the peg**: banking, customs, the railway, the
cross-border trade with Valdória. Whether Esmeralda is counted as a seat of its own or as a
territory of the Verdenese standard is **open**; the Spanish-zone Verdenese of Korentē and Misjôn
(`../verdenese/regional-varieties.md` §2) is the nearest written register and the likely model.

## The Andean line

The connection to Valdória is a railway: the **Andean line** runs north from Valdória's western
corridor, over the **Huhūi passes**, to Susana, with a branch to Baca. It is the freight route the
peg is paid in, and the line a Valdórian who wants to be somewhere else at midnight takes. The
ground between Valdória's written provinces and Esmeralda's is **open**: the founder may extend
either state's territory so the passes are a true border, or leave the line to run through
ground that belongs to neither.

## Kinship

With Valdória: the two South American Nelosphere states, one founded by a Nelôxian company and
settled by Europeans, one brought in by money. With Zaryanova and Valdória both: the
founder's pattern of real territory, fictionalized, kept.
