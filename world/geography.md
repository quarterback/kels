# Nelôxia — Geographic Footprint (settled)

A maritime-infrastructure great power in connected theatres, running from the White Sea through the
Baltic, Black Sea, Adriatic, and western Mediterranean and overseas to the Red Sea and the Indian
Ocean — the **twelve regions plus the Yemeni Commonwealth and the federal city of Sevastopol** of
[`boundaries.md`](boundaries.md) (the authoritative map layer; this file is the physical read of
it). Structural scale and posture are in
[`modern-neloxia-working-lore-model.md`](modern-neloxia-working-lore-model.md); identity in
[`neloxia-lore.md`](neloxia-lore.md); history in
[`neloxia-expanded-lore.md`](neloxia-expanded-lore.md); the searchable settlement roster is
[`settlements.html`](../settlements.html). History/politics remain founder-controlled.

## The theatres

- **The Northern lobe (Region 1)** — the Republic of Karelia and the Leningrad approaches:
  isthmus gate Vīpôri, lake-capital Petrôsô, White Sea coast Korbitô. Boreal timber, lakes, and
  the Arctic frontage that makes Nelôxia a two-ocean transit state. Sparse because it is cold,
  but integral — "our own Norway without the oil."
- **The Baltic core (Regions 2–4)** — the state's archive core and a large share of its mass. The
  Livonian islands (Sārô, Hiumō) and the southeastern Estonian–Latvian interior; the Lithuanian
  spine from Māmeli's harbor through the Kaunô basin (Keidani); and the Prussian–Pomeranian shore
  belt — the capital basin at **Kunislinnô**, the delta crown Tantsika, the Oder gate Stetīn. One
  continuous trading shore from the Curonian Spit to the Oder mouth.
- **The eastern corridor (Regions 5–6)** — the Belarus–Lublin seam (Marīsô, the rail-gauge
  transfer at Volkovixi) running south through Subcarpathia and Prešov into Transcarpathia, the
  Moldavian arc, and the Black Sea littoral: the Danube-mouth terminal Kôstônç and the
  founding-diaspora anchor **Uusatôm**. The Baltic–Black Sea axis, the old river-and-rail road
  between the seas — with the naval federal city of **Sevastopol** on the Crimean shore.
- **The south-western arm (Regions 7–8)** — the Pannonian bridge (nine Hungarian counties, the
  land-link between the arms) rising into the Alpine hinge at Gräts and dropping to the Adriatic
  at **Triest**, the legal-financial southern port, with the Slovene coast-karst and Goriška.
- **The Dalmatian coast (Region 9)** — the Istrian peninsula, the Kvarner (**Fiume**, Pôla, Krk),
  and the seaboard south of the arm: Zadar and Šibenik's waters, Spalôt, Ragūz, and the
  Herzegovinian hinterland cantons. The warm-water naval-commercial shore.
- **The western Alpine–Riviera arc (Region 10)** — the industrial heart of the expanded state: the
  Rhône–Riviera coast (Marsēl, Nissô, Tolon), the **Lombard–Piedmont basin** (Milān, Torīnô), the
  **Veneto–Adriatic belt** (Venēsiô, Verona, Padova), and the southern Swiss valleys (Lugano,
  Sion, Chur) at the passes. The Po-valley manufacturing core and the Mediterranean terminals.
- **The Thracian–Macedonian corridor (Region 11)** — the southern Balkan land-bridge to the
  Aegean threshold: the Bulgarian south (Burgas), Eastern Thrace at Edirne, the southern Albanian
  coast (Valonô), and the Macedonian Vardar (Monastir, Veles).
- **The Yemeni Commonwealth (Region 12)** — the transoceanic constituent state on the Red Sea and
  the Indian Ocean: the federal gateway **Greater Aden**, the highland capital **Sana'a**, Taiz,
  Hodeidah, Ibb, and the Hadhramaut. Aden is the southern counterpart to Kunislinnô — the state's
  Bab-el-Mandeb anchor. *(See [`yemeni-commonwealth.md`](yemeni-commonwealth.md).)*

The state is held together the way it was assembled — by sea-lanes, rivers, rail, customs law, and
(for Yemen) an ocean crossing as much as by land adjacency: accumulated jurisdictions, not a drawn
ethnic border (`neloxia-expanded-lore.md`).

## The maritime spine (why the language is what it is)

Rivers — the Nemunas through the spine, the upper Daugava reach, the Danube mouth in the south, and
now the Po across the western core — float interior bulk goods (timber, grain, flax, hemp, and the
Po valley's manufactures) down to the coastal lagoons and terminals, where cargo is broken-of-bulk
and re-manifested onto deepwater ships. Because everything is logged and taxed at the
counting-houses in dozens/gross/long-hundreds, the **Hanseatic base-12 system saturated the whole
culture, interior included** — the physical reason the language counts the way it does. The loan
strata map to physical contacts: Low German at the Baltic ports, Slavic at the Marīsô
river-bottleneck and down the eastern corridor, Scandinavian on the long northern runs to Karelia,
Venetian and Romance down the Adriatic and across the western arc, Ottoman and Greek along the
southern corridor, and Arabic across the Red Sea at Aden.
