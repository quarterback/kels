# Nelôxi Grammar — Sounds and Spelling

*Foundation module 05. The sound inventory, its IPA values, and the rules above the segment.
Everything here was already operating in the coursebook and the orthographic rulings; this module
states it in one place.*

---

## The letters

**a b d e f g h i j k l m n o p r s t u v x y ä ö ô ç ñ**

plus the long vowels **ā ē ī ō ū ǟ ȫ**, the palatal **ļ**, the word-final **õ**, and the geminate
**l·l**.

There is no **c**, **q**, **w** or **z** in the native inventory.

## Vowels

| Letter | IPA | As in | Long |
|---|---|---|---|
| **a** | /a/ | f**a**ther | **ā** /aː/ |
| **e** | /e/ | b**e**t | **ē** /eː/ |
| **i** | /i/ | mach**i**ne | **ī** /iː/ |
| **o** | /o/ | n**o**te | **ō** /oː/ |
| **u** | /u/ | r**u**le | **ū** /uː/ |
| **ä** | /æ/ | c**a**t | **ǟ** /æː/ |
| **ö** | /ø/ | rounded *e*, lips forward | **ȫ** /øː/ |
| **y** | /y/ | rounded *i*, lips forward | — |
| **ô** | /ɤ/ | an unrounded *o*, back of the mouth, relaxed lips | — |

**ô is the characteristic sound of the language** and the letter the orthography is named for.

**Length is contrastive.** A macron means the vowel is held twice as long, and the difference
carries meaning: *tule* is not *tulē*.

**õ** is the living word-final ending (*kaunõ*, *sagõ*, *harvõ*) — it does not appear elsewhere.

**No nasal vowels.** Front rounded vowels (**ö y ȫ**) are present and are a Finnic inheritance —
typologically unusual for a language of this shape, and one of the clearest marks of the substrate.

## Consonants

Pronounced as written, with these values:

| Letter | IPA | As in |
|---|---|---|
| **ç** | /ts/ | ca**ts** |
| **x** | /ʃ/ | **sh**ip |
| **ñ** | /ɲ/ | ca**ny**on |
| **j** | /j/ | **y**es |
| **ļ** | /lʲ/ | palatal *l* — *kēļ*, *tūļ* |
| **h** | /h/ | always pronounced, never silent |
| **l·l** | /lː/ | a long held *l* — *famīl·la* |

**Doubled consonants** (*tt kk ll ss nn*) are held longer and the difference matters.

### The ceremonial x

**x** reads /ʃ/ throughout the ordinary lexicon. In the proper-name register it reads **/ks/** —
the archaic-x, charter §47. *Nelôxi* is /neˈlɤksi/; *xolkô* is /ˈʃolkɤ/. Context decides, and the
register is the context.

## Above the segment

**Stress falls on the first syllable of every word, without exception.** No word in the language
is stressed anywhere else. This is why unstressed syllables erode so readily — everything after
the first is a candidate for reduction, syncope or loss.

**Vowel harmony** governs helper and suffix vowels:

| | Vowels |
|---|---|
| **Back** | a · o · u · ô |
| **Front** | ä · ö · y · e · i |

A suffix takes **-ô-** after a back-vowel stem and **-e-** after a front-vowel stem: *kōtôl* "at the
house", *kēļel* "in the tongue".

## What cannot begin a word

Six letters never occur word-initially: **y · ö · ñ · ļ · õ · l·l**. They appear only inside a word
— and because stress is always initial, **y** and **ö** in a first syllable are stressed nuclei
(*kylä*, *köis*) while **ñ ļ õ** fall in unstressed positions.

This is a real constraint, not a gap in the dictionary, and it is what the spelling alphabet
(`16-radio-alphabet.md`) has to work around.

## Scar letters

Loans may keep letters the native inventory does not have, worn openly rather than sanded off:

| Scar | Value | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **ž · dž** | /ʒ/ · /dʒ/ | *dīdžē* |
| **w** | /w/ | *wifī* |
| **œ** | /ø/ | *flœzī* |
| **š** | /ʃ/ | grandfathered only — *krašô*; new coinages use **x** |

Also permitted in loans: **f**, **sch**, **z**, **sz**.

## The orthographic law

**î, û and â exist in neither the source languages nor Nelôxi. They are errors, not fossils.**
Only **ô** and the macrons **ā ē ī ō ū ǟ ȫ**. A form carrying î/û/â is a mistake to be corrected,
never a variant to be preserved.
